# A1-C1 分级测试（英语等级评估）

用于学员不知道或不确定自己 CEFR 等级时的评估。15 道单选题，五档（A1/A2/B1/B2/C1）各 3 题。

## 使用方式

1. 在对话中一次给出全部 15 题（编号 + 选项），让学员在一条消息里作答（可只写编号+答案字母）。
2. 学员作答后按「评分规则」定级。
3. 建议同时让学员 30 秒口语自我介绍（录音）或写 3-5 句英文，交叉验证，尤其对 B2/C1 结果。
4. 定级后按 Skill 主流程记录偏好并开始选片段。

## 评分规则

- 每档 3 题，答对 ≥2 题视为「通过该档」。
- 最终等级 = 连续通过的最高档；若中间某档未通过，退回到断档前一档（例如 A2 没通过但 B1 对了 2 题，等级仍为 A1）。
- 答案位于本文件末尾，出题时不要展示。

## 题目

### A1 档（入门）
1. ___ name is Tom.
   A. I  B. My  C. Me  D. Mine
2. She ___ to school every day.
   A. go  B. goes  C. going  D. gone
3. — How are you? — ___.
   A. I'm fine, thanks.  B. I'm Tom.  C. It's Monday.  D. Yes, I am.

### A2 档（基础）
4. I ___ TV when you called me.
   A. watch  B. watched  C. was watching  D. watches
5. There are ___ apples on the table.
   A. some  B. any  C. much  D. a
6. — Where ___ you born? — In Shanghai.
   A. are  B. were  C. do  D. have

### B1 档（中级）
7. If it ___ tomorrow, we'll stay home.
   A. rains  B. will rain  C. rained  D. raining
8. The book ___ by a famous writer.
   A. writes  B. was written  C. is writing  D. wrote
9. I look forward to ___ from you soon.
   A. hear  B. hearing  C. heard  D. be heard

### B2 档（中高级）
10. If I ___ you, I would take the job.
    A. am  B. was  C. were  D. be
11. Not only ___ late, but he also forgot his keys.
    A. he was  B. was he  C. he is  D. is he
12. The report needs ___ by Friday.
    A. to finish  B. finishing  C. finished  D. be finished

### C1 档（高级）
13. Hardly ___ the station when the train left.
    A. had I reached  B. I had reached  C. have I reached  D. I reached
14. The negotiations broke ___ after weeks of talks.
    A. up  B. down  C. off  D. out
15. She is ___ to her employees, which is why they respect her.
    A. condescending  B. contemptuous  C. considerate  D. complacent

## 答案

A1: 1-B, 2-B, 3-A ｜ A2: 4-C, 5-A, 6-B ｜ B1: 7-A, 8-B, 9-B ｜ B2: 10-C, 11-B, 12-B ｜ C1: 13-A, 14-B, 15-C

## 定级后的起点建议

- A1/A2：选 20-40 秒、词汇极简的片段（问候、点餐、日常动作），解析侧重基础词汇与简单句型。
- B1：30-60 秒、日常对话、有 2-4 处连读弱读点（本 Skill 默认适配档）。
- B2：45-75 秒、可含习语、虚拟语气、略快语速。
- C1：60-90 秒、复杂从句、文化梗密集、语速快。
